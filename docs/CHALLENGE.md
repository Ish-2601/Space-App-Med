# Challenge summary (from the official page)

Build a **visual tool** that helps astronauts **assess their own health** during missions and gives **guidance on treatments and health improvements**.

## Must address
- Gather health indicators (any input: text, clicks, camera). Examples: blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar, temperature, sleep hours, mood.
- Present metrics and the assessment clearly (UI design matters; animation and interactivity welcome).
- Don't assume astronauts know how to assess their health: give simple instructions and explanations.

## Questions to answer in our write-up
1. Which measurements can be performed in space?
2. Why is each exam needed?
3. How do spaceflight hazards affect each indicator?
4. When does a result warrant attention from the crew or ground flight surgeons?
5. What actions can prevent a critical condition?

## Optional ideas from the page
Clickable body figure, gamification, dynamic charts, color coding, periodic measurement prompts, different environments (spacecraft, station, rover, planetary base).

## Hazards (NASA HRP)
Space radiation, isolation and confinement, distance from Earth, gravity fields, hostile closed environments.

## Rules we follow
- Label simulated data clearly.
- Cite a source for every threshold and recommendation.
- State it's a prototype, not medical advice.
